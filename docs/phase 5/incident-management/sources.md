## Source limitations

- Session 1 and 2 pasted transcripts do not establish calendar dates; session 2 ends at a lunch break. The opening of session 3 recaps later decisions without supplying that missing continuation.
- The session 3 and 4 document headers are dated 4 November 2026, with displayed times 02:34pm and 05:37pm respectively; no timezone is inferred. Their drafting/revision dates are not formal approval dates.
- Word extractions retain all 502 session 3 and 363 session 4 body paragraphs. Room-speaker labels may represent several people. No formal decider or approval is inferred from those labels.
- Referenced SOWs, field inventories, process diagrams, clinical procedures, and coordinate examples were not supplied. Product and regulatory statements remain workshop evidence, not independent validation.
