## 1. Design consequences and source precedence

First release includes automated operational BOMEX/ECS intake and controlled manual fallback, internal initial drawing review, explicit engineer issue, independent element execution, evidence reuse, approvals, security, reporting, and certificates. PCR and inspection work are PPAP-side interfaces. Shipment integration, recurring post-approval validation, and a dedicated TACT-TRI build are deferred (`PPAP-SCOPE-001`, `PPAP-INT-001`). Existing APQP behavior remains governed by its own configuration.

Target behavior on PPAP records and their operational children applies only to PPAPs with the system-controlled `Target Configuration?=TRUE` discriminator. Existing PPAP records use `FALSE`; preserve their retained baseline behavior and the existing Project Framework inheritance. PPAP actions, reminders, reporting populations, certificate generation, and changed shared-framework handler branches must apply that target guard. Pre-PPAP operational intake and initial drawing review use their own target-object, source-service and role authorization; they do not require an already-existing PPAP. Requirement/template publication likewise uses its own governed configuration authorization. Do not apply the target workflow or reporting interpretation to existing APQP or retained baseline PPAP work.

The latest PPAP decisions override conflicting historical wording. A new engineering change requiring PPAP creates a new PPAP whether the earlier one is open or closed; the older open PPAP normally continues against its original revision. Do not absorb that change as supplemental engineering context or reopen an approved element because of it. The initial drawing-review gate and governed no-PPAP exceptions remain. An approved element in an open PPAP may still be corrected for a non-engineering reason; its prior approved snapshot remains reusable subject to compatibility and source-cancellation rules (`PPAP-CHANGE-001/002`, `PPAP-EVIDENCE-004`).

Each approval concerns one manufacturing facility and one drawing with explicit included parts. Shipping depots provide separate routing context and do not replace that facility or create additional approvals merely because a depot differs. Each model-change PPAP has one governing model/program and one manufacturing stage. Criticality flags and visibility classification are different concepts: imported important-safety, important-quality, and regulated flags govern requirements; security visibility governs who may see a record (`PPAP-SCOPE-002`, `PPAP-SCHEDULE-001`, `PPAP-CLASSIFICATION-001/002/003`).

### 2.1 Exact role resolution

For each supplier-facing assignment, select the relevant linked depot or other explicitly governed exact Supplier Abstract context, and the required Supplier Management Role. Resolve exactly one Supplier Specific Role whose `Supplier Abstract` and `Supplier Management Role` are those records. Require current eligible active Members before issue or assignment. Zero matches, multiple matches, or no eligible member blocks the action with an actionable configuration message.

The same rule applies to internal supplier-specific role resolution using the relevant exact supplier context and governed model-change, running-change, or process-change role. A company-scoped role never satisfies a facility/depot requirement. A contact with legitimate parent-company access may be eligible as a member of a separate facility/depot group; this is not inheritance of the company role. Standalone supplier-created roles cannot silently satisfy a standard library-role requirement. See the [role specification's workflow and hierarchy contract](../../supplier-relationship-management/solution_specification/object_model/supplier-management-role-logical-model.md#supplier-hierarchy-and-role-resolution) and [ADR-026](../ADR-026-route-ppap-work-through-stable-supplier-depot-and-internal-roles.md).

Proposed PPAP multi-member behavior is all eligible current members notified, with the first authorized successful action completing the workflow action. Record the individual actor. Internal inspection-team intake may be reassigned to an individual by an authorized team leader. Membership maintenance remains in SRM. Native dynamic membership effects on open work require tenant verification; if recipients are snapshotted, governed reassignment must preserve former assignee and action history.

### 2.2 Central lifecycle use

Apply capabilities for the exact manufacturing facility and its Supplier Parent Company, together with capability restrictions on any entity participating in routed work. `SERVICE` permits service-related PPAP work under `PPAP-ELIGIBILITY-001` and denies new mass-production work. `INACTIVE` denies operational participation while retaining centrally authorized historical visibility. Missing policy or status is an owned configuration exception, not implicit permission. Label service context explicitly in the PPAP contract; do not infer it from a depot name.

Status changes do not automatically cancel PPAP or change its issued scope. Consume the central open-work/status-transition policy. The status specification still marks its detailed open-work impact-blocking step for review while other prose proposes blocking restrictive transitions. The final capability identifiers, PPAP impact adapter, and whether that central blocker is enabled remain implementation decisions; PPAP must not silently resolve that source discrepancy by introducing its own transition or cancellation behavior.

## 3. Separate operational BOMEX/ECS intake

### 3.1 Isolation is preserved

The existing **Product Integration** application owns exactly these eleven local objects: BOMECS Part API Recod, Program Reference, Supplier Reference, Part Revision Reference, Product Family, Part Flag, Part Tag, Part Supplier Relationship, Supplier Relationship Role, Engineering Change Number, and Engineering Change Scope. Their [technical specification §§1–3](../../product-management/solution_specification/object_model/product-management-api-technical-specification.md#1-purpose-and-application-boundary) forbids existing-object relationships, inheritance, lookups, automatic matching, synchronization and writebacks. None is an operational PPAP or supplier master connection.

Do not add PPAP fields, workflows, selectors, triggers, inverse grids, lookup relationships, or matching from those eleven objects to operational objects. Do not infer drawing identity from isolated Product Family, authoritative criticality from Part Flag, manufacturing-facility eligibility from Include All Facilities?, or source release from local Active?. Do not reuse the isolated source tuples as operational keys without a separate producer contract. The separate integration may receive data from the same upstream system; that fact does not connect the isolated component to PPAP.

### 3.2 Proposed operational connection contract

SIA's integration owner supplies a distinct operational BOMEX/ECS channel, with dedicated operational mappings and authenticated least-privilege service access. Reuse native API, audit, execution diagnostics, source-to-Intelex identifier mapping and retry facilities where sufficient. This document does not prescribe new integration business objects. A complete payload, endpoint, schema, native staging mechanism, and operational target-object inventory have not been supplied.

Within the PPAP target, PPAP Engineering Release retains applicable source context and PPAP Drawing Review carries its determination. PPAP Part and PPAP Shipping Depot implement explicit parts and separate depot links; PPAP Model Change, PPAP Model Date and PPAP Build Event carry PPAP schedule/event context. Their exact field, provenance and relationship contracts belong to the [target field contract](ppap-target-field-contract.md). These PPAP-owned names do not identify the external operational master objects or authorize links to isolated Product Integration records.

The following are **semantic requirements**, not proposed JSON property names or deployed endpoints. Every wire key, source namespace, identifier format, and operational target-field binding is **Open** until agreed with SIA's producer and operational Product Management owner.

A correction to classifications applicable to the issued PPAP's own source revision requires cancellation and replacement while it is open. Combine all applicable requirements across included parts; any important-safety part requires an explicit management approval, while important-quality or regulated flags alone do not trigger that approval (`PPAP-CLASSIFICATION-001/002/003`, `PPAP-CHANGE-001/002`).

### 3.3 Processing and failure contract

1. Validate producer authorization, source identity, delivery version and required references before operational mutation. Retain native receipt/error evidence. Unknown operational targets or ambiguous crosswalks enter an administrator-owned reconciliation queue; they do not default to a similarly named object.
2. Apply the agreed idempotency and ordering rule under concurrency control. Replays do not create duplicate source contexts or initial drawing reviews; stale updates do not replace newer confirmed context. Exact uniqueness and correction keys require producer confirmation before production writes.
3. Persist complete operational source context and then create or resolve the initial drawing-review record for each applicable released drawing context. Never create an issued PPAP directly from intake. A failure leaves no partially issued work; verify native transaction/staging and retry behavior during configuration.
4. Route review through the governed internal role and require the PPAP determination and applicable inspection-specification checkpoint. A governed no-PPAP outcome retains reviewer, timestamp, reason and source lineage. Missing source classifications may be reconciled during preparation, but must block issue.
5. A PPAP-required decision launches a new internal draft carrying the verified scope; only the responsible engineer's explicit Issue action makes it supplier-visible. For a different new engineering change, “addressed by another PPAP” cannot absorb that change into an existing PPAP.
6. Source delivery alone does not change an issued PPAP's locked facility, drawing, revision, included parts, classifications or requirement set. Apply target cancellation/replacement rules for changes to the issued applicable context. Preserve the previous engineering context and retained histories.

Controlled manual initiation is a proposed permission for the responsible internal PPAP engineer and designated PPAP administrator, within ordinary record scope. Record its manual provenance, reason and available source context; perform the same drawing-review, classification, requirement, scope and issue checks. Manual fallback does not authorize manual authoritative-flag overrides. When delayed automated delivery arrives, reconcile to the existing source-linked review/draft using the approved source crosswalk and human resolution where ambiguous; do not duplicate or automatically merge issued PPAPs. Exact manual-initiation grant and reconciliation keys remain open under `PPAP-INT-001`.

## 5. Security contract

### 5.1 Authorization dimensions

Authorization requires the intersection of authenticated account eligibility, application/object/record permissions, supplier-entity scope, visibility classification, lifecycle capability, and current stage/action eligibility. Assignment or notification recipient status is insufficient by itself. Validate again inside mutating actions, including direct URLs, API requests and attachment access.

Supplier entity scope derives from governed Supplier Contact relationships: one facility, explicitly related facilities, or company scope including its facilities under [ADR-078](../../supplier-relationship-management/adrs/ADR-078-scope-supplier-portal-access-through-supplier-entity-relationships.md). Mandatory SIA Location values are administrative and do not supply this boundary. Depot routing does not grant manufacturing-facility access. Before issue, verify that selected depot-role members can access the manufacturing-facility-scoped PPAP through the approved entity authorization mapping; unresolved mappings must not create an implicit company-wide grant.

Visibility follows [ADR-084](../../supplier-relationship-management/adrs/ADR-084-apply-mutable-visibility-classifications-to-supplier-related-records.md): new-model-authorized users may see applicable mass-production history; mass-production-only users cannot see restricted new-model records. Apply the PPAP context to tasks, evidence, discussions and PPAP-scoped inspection descendants. Keep shared consignment headers from bridging contexts. Final visibility catalogue codes, named security groups and native ACL bindings remain to be supplied; the following matrix uses business roles and does not assert that those groups already exist.

### 5.2 Stage and role matrix

The target PPAP stages are **Draft**, **In Progress**, and **Management Review**, with **Approved** and **Cancelled** termination outcomes. PPAP Element stages are **Work In Progress** and **Engineer Review**, with **Approved** and **Cancelled** termination outcomes. Initial drawing review has its separate native workflow. There is full approval only: target reports, views and exports must not expose Interim Approval as a target outcome (`PPAP-LIFECYCLE-001`). The matrix groups equivalent authorization checks across those stages; the [technical specification](ppap-target-technical-specification.md) owns action ordering. `View` always means authorized fields and attachments only. No ordinary role may hard-delete retained PPAP evidence, alter an immutable PPAP Evidence Snapshot, override imported criticality, or edit locked issued scope.

Part masses are a separate final-submission gate.

### 5.3 Discussion, file and output boundaries

Both internal and authorized supplier users may start supplier-visible threads and reply while PPAP remains open. Internal-only threads require internal authorization. Adopt the accepted [ADR-107](../ADR-107-model-ppap-discussions-as-secured-threaded-related-records.md) fallback of immutable thread-level internal/supplier-visible classification; replies inherit the thread audience. Each posted comment requires nonblank text context, and files may accompany it. Internal content cannot be reclassified as supplier-visible by moving a reply or changing recipients. Notification targeting does not narrow a thread's actual audience.

Posted comments and attached files are append-only; corrections use new replies. Approval or cancellation makes all discussion read-only for internal and supplier users, including new threads/replies/files. Retain original author, posting timestamp, reply context and file lineage. Pre-post preparation remains editable. Attachments require authorization to their owning PPAP/task/thread and their own internal/supplier visibility rule. Search, previews, exports, download links and notifications must not expose hidden filenames, text, internal rationale or management exchange content.

Generate the controlled PDF certificate only after actual successful final approval; include actual actor/timestamp and approved scope from retained data. Provide individual evidence downloads only for files that the user may already access. Consolidated evidence-package download and approval withdrawal are excluded from first release. Retain records, rejected and approved evidence, discussions and certificates with no automatic deletion. English is the launch language; stable keys remain distinct from future localizable labels (`PPAP-OUTPUT-001`, `PPAP-RETENTION-001`, `PPAP-LOCALIZATION-001`).

### 5.4 Visibility handover versus source correction

Management-controlled new-model to mass-production handover changes access on the relevant model summary, PPAPs, tasks and governed descendants. It may occur while work is open. Audit the change; do not move work, reset approvals, change due dates or transfer responsibility. Validate the complete cascade and report partial failures as actionable exceptions without exposing affected records prematurely. Native inherited classification or equivalent secured rollout must be verified before enabling bulk handover.

This visibility handover is independent of important-safety/important-quality/regulated classification changes. Those source changes follow §3.2 and cancellation/replacement controls. No visibility action may waive criticality-driven evidence or management approval.

## 6. Reporting and metric definitions

### 6.1 Live operational reporting

Use native workflow instances joined to retained PPAP scope, source, assignment and date facts. Do not infer open/completed/approved from a text status field or introduce reporting-only workflow copies. Each work item appears once per applicable native workflow instance, with PPAP and element identity retained to prevent join multiplication. All detail respects §5 security. Aggregations must not expose restricted identifiers through drilldown, export or notification links.

| Report | Grain and measures | Filters and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Initial drawing-review queue | One source-linked review; release date, review due date, decision timestamp, current reviewer group/individual, no-PPAP reason and missing source/routing exceptions | Source release/revision, drawing, candidate facility, model/program, change context; due at release + 14 calendar days |
| PPAP work queue | One active native PPAP or element stage instance; current assignee group, individual assignee if established, due date, stage age, overdue days | Facility, linked depot, drawing/revision/part, model/program, manufacturing stage, source/change type, engineer, workflow stage, visibility domain |
| Work completion / approval history | One actual workflow decision or termination; actor, role/group, decision and time; completed durations and retained due dates | Distinguish assigned group from actual actor; reassignment never attributes prior completed work to current group members |
| Due-date change and schedule impact | One audited date change; prior/current effective date, immutable issued baseline, actor/time/reason, model connection and reconnection warning | Show completed dates untouched; current-date changes must not overwrite prior first-submission comparison |
| Required-result readiness | One explicitly required inspection/run-at-rate element; linked result/evidence and engineer acceptance | Do not label unrelated open pilot work as a PPAP approval blocker |
| Evidence reuse and correction | One approved evidence version and receiving snapshot lineage; compatibility confirmation, alternate-version reason, source cancellation and correction status | Cancelled source is excluded from future reuse; prior approved version remains eligible during permitted open-PPAP correction; existing copied snapshots remain retained |
| Cancellations | One cancelled PPAP/element with reason, actor/time and whether first complete supplier submission existed | Expose unresolved PCR/APQP linkage separately; cancellation never counts as approval |
| Source / security administration | One owned failure or exception from native integration, mapping, role, status, classification or handover evidence | Restricted internal administration; missing source data or permissions is not supplier lateness |

For date-based work, due today is not overdue until the business date exceeds the due date. Overdue days are nonnegative calendar-day differences. Materially overdue means at least 7 calendar days overdue initially, matching configured escalation; show the configured threshold on the report. Stage review targets initially use submission/resubmission plus 5 calendar days, separately for engineer review and final approval. Reminder defaults are due minus 3 days, due date, weekly while overdue; supplier escalation goes to the engineer after 7 overdue days, internal/review/final approval escalation to PPAP administrators. These controls never auto-approve, auto-cancel or auto-reassign (`PPAP-SCHEDULE-002`, `PPAP-REVIEW-001`, `PPAP-NOTIFICATION-001`).

### 6.2 Supplier timeliness fact contract

The metric unit is the supplier-completed element on one PPAP, not one attachment, included part, reviewer, linked program, or submission attempt. Native submission/action history supplies events; the field contract supplies any retained facts required where native evidence cannot freeze the applicable deadline. The following are required report semantics rather than additional field declarations.

| Fact / measure | Exact meaning |
|---|---|
| First complete supplier submission | Earliest successful submission of that supplier element that passes the required-field and file checks. Incomplete attempts do not qualify. Engineer acceptance or final approval is not this event |
| Original baseline deadline | Element due date at first issue, retained unchanged even if a connected model schedule later changes |
| Operational submission deadline | Effective operational due date at the first complete supplier submission, frozen with that submission. Later date changes do not change this comparison |
| Baseline on time? | First complete supplier submission's business calendar date is on or before original baseline deadline |
| Operational on time? | Same submission date is on or before frozen operational submission deadline |
| Baseline / operational lateness days | For each deadline separately, maximum of zero and submission date minus deadline in calendar days. Keep both measures and their actual dates |
| Internal review duration | Submission/resubmission to the corresponding engineer or management decision. Report separately from supplier timeliness; internal delay cannot change the supplier submission event |
| Rejection and rework | Count decisions returning/rejecting submitted evidence and subsequent complete resubmissions; report review cycles separately. Do not replace the first-submission timeliness event with the eventual accepted resubmission |
| Cancellation before first complete submission | Exclude from supplier-timeliness denominator; retain and report cancellation separately |
| Cancellation after first complete submission | Retain the first-submission facts and both outcomes; report cancellation alongside them |
| New engineering change | New PPAP and new metric identity. Do not create an engineering-reopening cycle inside the old element |
| Reused approved evidence / internal element / inapplicable requirement | No new supplier-submission event solely because evidence is copied or internal work completes. Exclude from a supplier-submission denominator unless actual new supplier work is explicitly required; report the disposition rather than inventing a timestamp |

Date calculations require one configured PPAP business reporting timezone and a consistent day boundary; action timestamps retain timezone. The site business timezone is an implementation binding to confirm, not inferred from the author's workstation. Submission on its due date is on time. Do not substitute a midnight UTC comparison for a calendar-date rule.

### 6.3 Report populations and aggregation

Provide a **submission-event report** for period analysis: select qualifying first complete submissions whose timestamp falls within the requested reporting interval, then show the two independent punctuality counts and percentages over that same population. Exclude internal/no-new-submission dispositions, incomplete attempts, and work cancelled before any complete submission. A denominator of zero displays no measured submissions, not 100% or 0%. This report describes submitted work; it is not a measure of outstanding nonresponse.

Provide a separate **due-work report** at an explicit as-of timestamp: issued supplier elements with a due date at or before the as-of date, excluding internal/no-new-submission dispositions and unsubmitted cancelled work. Show unsubmitted outstanding/overdue counts, first submitted on time, first submitted late, and cancellation-after-submission counts. The immutable baseline view cohorts work by original issued due date; the operational view uses frozen operational deadline once submitted and the current applicable date for unsubmitted work. Label those bases clearly and expose date changes. Unsubmitted work is pending or overdue, never silently omitted from this due-work view or labelled as submitted late.

These are proposed reporting populations that make missing submissions visible while preserving the confirmed first-submission comparisons. They do not choose the scorecard's period cohort, point scale, weighting, materiality thresholds, or how two deadline bases contribute to one KPI. The scorecard owner must select and version that mapping. Both deadline outcomes remain reportable; do not double-count an element merely to expose them. When rolling up, sum eligible numerator/denominator counts within the selected population rather than averaging supplier percentages. Preserve exact manufacturing-facility attribution; depot views do not duplicate the underlying element. Company reporting uses governed rollup rules and distinct source identities.

### 6.4 Supplier Scorecard exchange

Under [ADR-058](../../supplier-scorecard/ADR-058-build-supplier-scorecards-as-scheduled-kpi-snapshots-from-integrated-source-data.md), launch scorecard KPIs in direct entry and enable governed query collection only after the authoritative PPAP report, supplier context, reporting population and scoring logic are validated. PPAP readiness does not itself change scorecard Collection Method or publish new KPI configuration.

The [scorecard specification §§8.1–8.2](../../supplier-relationship-management/solution_specification/object_model/supplier-scorecard-technical-specification.md#8-kpi-configuration-and-versioning) owns exact fields `Source Application`, `Source Report Reference`, and `Source Calculation Description` on Supplier Scorecard KPI and their immutable version copies. Bind Source Application to the approved PPAP business application, Source Report Reference to the deployed governed report/query, and Source Calculation Description to the selected supplier scope, period cohort, exclusions, dual deadline handling, aggregation and points logic. These bindings are open; a descriptive label in this document is not an executable Intelex expression or report identifier.

Provide exact source PPAP/element identities, manufacturing facility, first-submission time, original deadline, frozen operational deadline, both outcomes, cancellation disposition and extraction/as-of time as authorized query evidence. Map to `Supplier Scorecard.Supplier` through the exact scored Supplier Abstract, not a name or assumed depot/company match. Depot-scored and company-scored results need an approved attribution/aggregation rule before automated capture. PPAP does not write external values directly into Scorecard KPI Value, mutate scorecard overrides, or overwrite a published scorecard.

Open query captures may refresh under scorecard rules. Published supplier/period/KPI results remain frozen until an authorized targeted refresh or override. Later PPAP cancellation, source correction or schedule change never automatically rewrites those snapshots. Keep queried, overridden and effective points, actor/time and internal rationale in the scorecard's own authoritative model.

Under [ADR-115](../../supplier-scorecard/ADR-115-separate-aggregate-scorecard-access-from-source-record-security-and-restrict-administration.md), authorized internal scorecard users may see the complete aggregate within supplier scope despite PPAP visibility restrictions. Drilldown always rechecks PPAP source security. The current exact native security-group names and supplier publication gate belong to [scorecard specification §16.1](../../supplier-relationship-management/solution_specification/object_model/supplier-scorecard-technical-specification.md#161-permission-contract); PPAP does not reuse those group memberships as PPAP View or approval grants. The query service must be authorized for its governed aggregate capture without exposing restricted source identifiers in supplier-visible KPI content.
