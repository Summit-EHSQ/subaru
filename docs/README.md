## Latest Revision Summary

- **Existing ADRs refined in this revision:** ADR-003, ADR-004, ADR-046, ADR-048 through ADR-053, ADR-056, ADR-059, and ADR-088.
- **New ADRs created in this revision:** ADR-092 through ADR-096.
- **Superseded ADRs:** ADR-001 is superseded by ADR-083; ADR-055 is superseded by ADR-087.
- **Status changes:** ADR-055 changed from proposed to superseded.
- **Total ADRs:** 96.
- **Current status distribution:** 74 accepted, 20 proposed, and 2 superseded ADRs.

### Material Refinements to Existing ADRs

- Supplier NCR uses an explicit parent-and-child structure with type-driven requirements, independent response reviews, role-based routing, and scheduled follow-up children.
- Released supplier NCRs cannot be transferred to another supplier; incorrect responsibility is handled through cancellation and recreation.
- WCAR now uses the shared supplier-issue workflow framework rather than a separately maintained end-to-end workflow.
- Warranty Analysis remains separate from NCR and now has claim-driven eligibility, dedicated evidence fields, month-end incomplete handling, and a concurrent integration dependency.
- SQDIIR and optional quality-CAR architecture remains valid, while initial-release scope is subject to schedule and budget confirmation.

### New Architectural Areas

- Role-qualified multiple-supplier relationships for parts.
- RMA as a disposition-linked child workflow with separate sort instructions.
- Simple record identifiers with normalized searchable business attributes.
- Proposed use of Meetings Management for supplier visits and 7-4 activity.
- Type-specific CAR determination with scored environmental escalation.
