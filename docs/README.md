### Material Refinements to Existing ADRs

- Product Management now composes a canonical part from BOMEX and PartsMaster while preserving source-specific history and precedence.
- Pilot inspections are initiated through PPAP build-event context and executed through structured shipments, receipt assignment, unit inspections, and engineer-reviewed failures.
- The primary inspection experience is connected and responsive; the earlier offline-document fallback is no longer part of the baseline.
- New-model and mass-production visibility is inherited from PPAP context and can change through management-controlled model handover.
- Preliminary ECS decisions are held in an initial drawing-review gate rather than counted as draft PPAPs.
- PPAP requirements now use governed, versioned templates with automatic evidence carry-forward and retained rejection history.
- Important-safety classification, rather than important-quality classification alone, activates overall group-leader approval.

### New Architectural Areas

- Effective-dated parent-child part hierarchies.
- Manual pilot-program initiation from staged PPAPs and discrete build events.
- Event-scoped supplier shipments with part-level evidence and receiving assignment.
- Pilot cutover without historical inspection migration.
- Model-change summary records with shared phase-and-stage schedules and controlled PPAP-level date overrides.
- A proposed separation between point-in-time PPAP evidence and recurring production validation records.
